Controls

Click a key slot, then press the new key. Backspace clears a slot, Esc cancels. When Player 2 is off, both sets control Player 1.

Gamepads: D-pad / left stick to move · A / RT fire · B / X / LT ability. First gamepad = Player 1, second = Player 2.
