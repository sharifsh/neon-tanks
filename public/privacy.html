Privacy Policy

Neon Tanks: Arena Battle · Effective October 28, 2026

Neon Tanks is made by Sharif Sharipov, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy covers the Neon Tanks app on iPhone, iPad and Android and the free version on this website. The short version: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game only sends what it needs to run online rooms and community maps, plus anything you write to me with the support form.

What the game sends, and why

DataWhenWhy
Your callsign (the nickname you type)When you create or join an online room, or publish a mapSo other players in the room see who is who, and so maps show their author
Gameplay data: tank class, controls you press, positions, scoresWhile you play in an online roomTo run the shared game. It is sent to the other players in your room and is not stored
Maps you publish: map name, layout and callsignOnly when you press "Publish map"So anyone can find and play them under Community Maps
A random session IDWhile you are in an online roomTo tell players apart. A new one is made each time you open the game; it isn't linked to you or your device
Support messages: your email address, the topic and your message, plus your device type, system version, language, screen size and time zoneOnly when you send the support formSo I can answer you and fix problems (see Support messages)

Solo games, the level editor and test play run entirely on your device and send nothing.

Support messages

When you send the support form (on the Support page, on this website or in the app), your message is stored in my support inbox and emailed to me so I can reply. I use your email address only to answer that message: no newsletters, no marketing, and it isn't shared with anyone. The device details help me reproduce problems. Messages are deleted within 12 months, or sooner if you ask. To stop spam, the form keeps a one-way code made from your network address for up to a day; the address itself isn't stored. You can always email me instead.

What stays on your device

Your callsign, tank class, chosen style, your last game settings (mode, map, difficulty and conditions), volume, joystick and zoom choices, control bindings and whether you own the full version are saved only on your device. Deleting the app (or clearing this website's data) removes them.

Online rooms and network addresses

Online rooms connect players directly to each other where possible, and otherwise relay the game through a server. As with any online game, the devices and servers involved see your device's network (IP) address while you are connected. Public connection-helper servers are used to set up those direct connections. I don't store network addresses or use them to identify you (the support form's spam check keeps only the one-way code described above). Room messages are not kept after the room closes.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. I never see your name, email or payment details. The app only asks the store whether your store account owns the unlock.

Service providers

The website, the online-room service, the community map list and the support inbox run on infrastructure from third-party hosting, database and email providers. They process the data above only to provide that service to me.

No tracking, no ads

The game doesn't track you across other apps or websites, doesn't show ads, and doesn't sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

Children

Neon Tanks doesn't ask for anyone's age or real name and collects no personal information beyond the callsign a player chooses, and the email address someone gives when they write to support. Players should pick a callsign that isn't their real name.

Removing a map or your data

To have a published map or a support message removed, or for any privacy question, use the support form or email trackmyreceipt@gmail.com with the map name or link. I'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
