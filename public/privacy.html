Privacy Policy

Neon Tanks: Arena Battle · Effective November 4, 2026

Neon Tanks is made by Sharif Sharipov, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy covers the Neon Tanks app on iPhone, iPad and Android and the free version on this website. The short version: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game only sends what it needs to run online rooms, community maps and the leaderboards, plus anything you write to me with the support form.

What the game sends, and why

Solo games, the level editor and test play run on your device. The only thing a solo game sends is its result for the leaderboards, and you can turn that off in Settings. Games for two players on one device, online rooms, the level editor's test play and games on your own or community maps don't post results.

Support messages

When you send the support form (on the Support page, on this website or in the app), your message is stored in my support inbox and emailed to me so I can reply. I use your email address only to answer that message: no newsletters, no marketing, and it isn't shared with anyone. The device details help me reproduce problems. Messages are deleted within 12 months, or sooner if you ask. To stop spam, the form keeps a one-way code made from your network address for up to a day; the address itself isn't stored. You can always email me instead.

Leaderboards

The leaderboards show each player's best solo result for each mode and difficulty, this week and of all time, with their callsign, the stage or wave they reached and their score. Anyone can see them, in the game and on this website. A week's results are deleted 13 weeks after it ends; all-time results stay until they are removed. You can remove all your results at any time with Settings → Remove my leaderboard results, or by writing to me with your callsign. To keep the boards fair, results that couldn't happen in a real game are refused, posting keeps a one-way code made from your network address for up to a day (the address itself isn't stored), and I may hide results that look like cheating or callsigns that are offensive.

What stays on your device

Your callsign, tank class, chosen style, your last game settings (mode, map, difficulty and conditions), volume, joystick and zoom choices, control bindings, whether leaderboard posting is on, your leaderboard player ID, results waiting to be posted while you're offline, and whether you own the full version are saved only on your device. Deleting the app (or clearing this website's data) removes them.

Online rooms and network addresses

Online rooms connect players directly to each other where possible, and otherwise relay the game through a server. As with any online game, the devices and servers involved see your device's network (IP) address while you are connected. Public connection-helper servers are used to set up those direct connections. I don't store network addresses or use them to identify you (the support form's spam check and the leaderboards' posting limit keep only the one-way codes described above). Room messages are not kept after the room closes.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. I never see your name, email or payment details. The app only asks the store whether your store account owns the unlock.

Service providers

The website, the online-room service, the community map list, the leaderboards and the support inbox run on infrastructure from third-party hosting, database and email providers. They process the data above only to provide that service to me.

No tracking, no ads

The game doesn't track you across other apps or websites, doesn't show ads, and doesn't sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

Children

Neon Tanks doesn't ask for anyone's age or real name and collects no personal information beyond the callsign a player chooses, and the email address someone gives when they write to support. Callsigns can appear in online rooms, on published maps and on the public leaderboards, so players should pick one that isn't their real name.

Removing a map or your data

Your leaderboard results can be removed in the game with Settings → Remove my leaderboard results. To have a published map, leaderboard results or a support message removed, or for any privacy question, use the support form or email trackmyreceipt@gmail.com with the map name or link, or your callsign. I'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
