Full version

What's free and what does the full version add?

Free: Classic co-op and Team Battle modes, Easy and Normal difficulty, 8 hand-made maps plus endless random maps, the Neon and Pixel styles, the Striker and Scout tanks, online rooms for up to 3 players, two players on one keyboard, and the level editor with test play.

Full version (one-time purchase): Survival, Capture the Flag, Deathmatch and Duel, Hard and Insane difficulty, all 24 maps, all 7 visual styles with their own sound and music, the Heavy and Engineer tanks, online rooms for up to 8 players, and publishing your maps to the community. No ads and no subscription.

How do I buy it?

In the app, tap ★ Full version at the bottom of the main screen, or tap anything with a 🔒. The price is shown on the button in your local currency. The purchase goes through the App Store (or Google Play) with your usual payment method.

I bought it on another device, or reinstalled the app. How do I get it back?

Tap Settings on the main screen, then Restore purchases (it's also on the full version card). Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. There's no charge to restore.

The button says "Not available yet" or shows no price.

The game couldn't reach the store. Check your internet connection and tap the button again. The game asks the store again every time the card opens.

It says my purchase is pending.

Some purchases need approval first, for example Ask to Buy or a slow payment method. The full version unlocks automatically as soon as the store confirms it. You don't need to buy again.

Do my friends need the full version to play with me?

No. In an online room, the host's copy decides what the room can use. If you host with the full version, up to 8 people can play in your room and everyone can use the full modes, maps, difficulty, styles and tanks. Their own copy stays as it is after they leave. A room hosted with the free version holds 3 players; anyone else who joins can watch as a spectator.

Is the full version shared with my family?

No. It belongs to the account that bought it and isn't shared through Family Sharing.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On iPhone or iPad, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open Google Play, then Payments & subscriptions, then Budget & history. After a refund, the game returns to the free version the next time it checks with the store.

Is the website different from the app?

The website is the free version. The full version is bought in the app.

Playing

How do I play with friends?

Tap Create Online Room and share the 5-letter room code (or invite link). Friends enter the code and tap Join. Rooms hold 8 players when the host has the full version (3 with the free version); extra people can watch as spectators. If the host leaves, another player takes over and the game keeps going.

Controls

On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen joystick and the FIRE and ABL (ability) buttons. The joystick stays at the bottom left; if you prefer it to appear wherever your thumb lands, choose Follows thumb in Settings (or in the pause menu). Game controllers and keyboards work too, and all keys can be changed under Settings → Keyboard & gamepad controls.

On a phone the view follows your tank. The magnifier button at the top right changes how close it is. The map buttons (at the top right, and the round one just above FIRE) show the whole map: tap for a quick look, or hold to keep it up until you let go.
